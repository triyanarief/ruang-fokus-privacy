Support

Help for the native Android focus timer

Liltgrove: Focus Timer works without an account or internet connection. For help with the app, email hello.triyan@gmail.com.

Start a focus session

On Home, tap Choose a task to select or add one, or leave it as a free session. Tap Start focus. You can change the duration for future sessions in Settings.

Pause, resume, or end early

Use the controls on the timer screen. Ending early asks for confirmation and saves the session with an Ended early label. You can add an optional note on the result screen. Closing and reopening the app should restore an active session from its saved timer state.

Session alerts

Alerts are optional. Turn them on in Settings and allow Android notification permission when prompted. If permission is denied, the timer continues to work. Android power management can delay a background alert, so reopening the app is the reliable way to check the saved session result.

Export or delete local data

In Settings, tap Export data and choose a destination in Android's file picker. The app saves a UTF-8 CSV of tasks and session history. To remove local app data, tap Delete all local data and confirm. Exported files stay at the destination you chose until you delete them there.
