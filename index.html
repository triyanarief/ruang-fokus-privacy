Liltgrove: Focus Timer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Liltgrove: Focus Timer is an offline focus timer for Android, developed by Triyan Arif Wibowo. You can use it without an account.

Information stored on your device

The app stores tasks you create, focus and break session history, optional session notes, the active timer, and your settings in private app storage on your Android device. This makes the timer and history work without an internet connection. The app does not request Android's internet permission and does not upload this information to us.

Notifications

If you choose to enable session alerts, the app may request Android notification permission. A local notification can show a task name when a session ends. Depending on your device's lock-screen settings, that text may be visible on the lock screen. You can turn alerts off in the app or change notification and lock-screen settings in Android. The timer works when alerts are disabled or permission is denied.

Export and deletion

If you tap Export data, Android's system file picker lets you choose where to save a UTF-8 CSV containing your tasks and session history. Your chosen destination may be a local folder or a service you use; that destination's privacy and backup settings apply to the exported file. The app does not export automatically.

You can delete the app's local tasks, sessions, and settings through the confirmed Delete all local data action in Settings. You can also remove local app data through Android's app storage controls or by uninstalling the app. Exported files are outside the app and must be deleted from their destination separately.

Backup, analytics, and third parties

The app disables Android app backup and device transfer of its private app data. Liltgrove: Focus Timer has no account login, advertising, analytics, or external data service. It does not include a third-party SDK that receives your tasks or sessions. Google Play and Android may separately process installation and diagnostic information under Google's own policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change. For questions or privacy requests, email hello.triyan@gmail.com.
